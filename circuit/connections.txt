The Power Foundation
5V Pin (Arduino) to Red Rail (+) on Breadboard

GND Pin (Arduino) to Blue Rail (-) on Breadboard

Power Bridge: Connect the Top Red Rail to Bottom Red Rail

Ground Bridge: Connect the Top Blue Rail to Bottom Blue Rail

1. Ultrasonic Sensor (Occupancy/Lights)
VCC to Red Rail (+)

GND to Blue Rail (-)

Trig to Digital Pin 9

Echo to Digital Pin 10

2. Gas Sensor (Odor Detection)
VCC to Red Rail (+)

GND to Blue Rail (-)

A0 (Signal) to Analog Pin A0

3. Leakage Sensor (Module + Probe)
VCC to Red Rail (+)

GND to Blue Rail (-)

A0 (Signal) to Analog Pin A1

Probe Connection: Use the 2 Female-to-Female wires to connect the "Fork" probe to the module pins.

4. IR Sensor (Touchless Tap)
VCC to Red Rail (+)

GND to Blue Rail (-)

OUT to Digital Pin 2

5. Buzzer (Safety Alarm)
Long Leg (+) to Digital Pin 8

Short Leg (-) to Blue Rail (-)

6. Room Light LED (Occupancy)
Long Leg (+) to Digital Pin 13

Short Leg (-) to Resistor to Blue Rail (-)

7. Leakage Warning LED
Long Leg (+) to Digital Pin 12

Short Leg (-) to Resistor to Blue Rail (-)

8. LCD Screen (The Display)
GND to Blue Rail (-)

VCC to Red Rail (+)

SDA to Analog Pin A4

SCL to Analog Pin A5
